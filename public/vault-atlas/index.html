What you get

  • Now Live

    Public events, S.C.O.R.E. season highlights, Minerva status, and this week’s nuke codes, with one-tap copy.

  • Widgets & Siri

    Nuke codes and a Minerva countdown on your Lock Screen. “Hey Siri, Vault Atlas nuke codes” or “What’s live in Vault Atlas.”

  • Offline search

    Look up perks, mutations, legendaries, events, locations, quests, and lore without leaving the game — including from Spotlight.

  • Meta starters

    Rotating build templates with SPECIAL, why-it’s-meta notes, and gear hints. Share a build with a vaultatlas:// code.

  • Fishing & quests

    An original Appalachia waters map, bait and weather guidance, a daily catch log, plus a quest log and Field Helper checkpoints.

  • Theorycraft Pack

    Optional $0.99 one-time unlock for the perk planner. Core features, including the Legendary Effects field guide, stay free.

Good to know

  • Vault Atlas is a fan-made, unofficial companion. It is not on the App Store yet; the public TestFlight beta is open now.
  • Game data and estimates can lag live patches. Always verify important values in-game.
  • The Theorycraft Pack is a $0.99 one-time in-app purchase. On current beta builds those tools are open so testers can try them.
