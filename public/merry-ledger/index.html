How it works

  1. Set your total holiday budget, then add the people on your list with a budget for each.
  2. Add gift ideas and prices. Anyone you're overspending on turns red.
  3. Tap Deals on a gift to compare prices and check its price history before you buy.
  4. Mark gifts bought, wrapped and given. Your shopping and to-wrap lists build themselves.
