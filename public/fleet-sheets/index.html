How it works

  1. Export your devices from your device management tool as a CSV or Excel file.
  2. Drag the file onto Fleet Sheets, or click Choose File.
  3. Fleet Sheets tells you what it found, such as "Jamf export · 70 devices".
  4. Click Create Report, pick where to save it, and open it in Excel.

What you get

  • It recognizes your export

    Fleet Sheets detects exports from Jamf Pro, Kandji, Microsoft Intune, and Addigy on its own.

  • Any other spreadsheet works too

    If it doesn't recognize a CSV, it asks which columns hold the computer name and serial number, plus optional macOS version and model.

  • A finished Excel report

    A summary sheet, formatted tables, a computers-by-OS chart, and Excel pivot tables you can filter. If a column is missing, that figure is left out.

  • Your files stay on your Mac

    Fleet Sheets does not connect to your MDM, does not call an API, and does not ask for credentials.

What the report looks like

Sheets from a report made with sample data. The rows are example data, not a real fleet.

Exports it reads

  • Jamf Pro: CSV or .xlsx
  • Kandji: Device Views CSV export
  • Microsoft Intune: the device list export from the portal, or the Graph Devices report
  • Addigy: the devices table export
  • Any other CSV or .xlsx, by matching the columns

The app's help shows how to make each export. Fleet Sheets documentation

Limitations

  • Microsoft Excel is required to open the report.
  • Needs macOS 13 or later. Runs on Intel and Apple silicon Macs.
  • Mosyle is not supported yet.
  • Kandji FileVault status is not in the Device Views export, so that figure is skipped for Kandji.
  • Other Kandji Prism or category exports are not supported. Other Intune reports are not supported.

Who it's for

Mac and MDM admins who already export a device list and want that file as a workbook. One organization per license. There is no MSP or multi-client tier. A second organization needs its own purchase.

FAQ

Does it connect to the MDM?
No. You drag in a file you already exported. Fleet Sheets does not use an API and does not need credentials.
Do I need to use Terminal?
No. Fleet Sheets is a regular Mac app. You drag it into Applications and open it.
