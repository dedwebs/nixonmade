NixonMade is Joe Nixon's shop. This page covers personal data for kits, services, and subscriptions sold on this site. Questions go to hello@nixonmade.com.

What we collect

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Purchase records: the product, the amount, the date, and the payment reference Stripe sends us

We receive your name and email when you check out or when you email us. Purchase records come from checkout.

Card payments

Stripe handles payments. We do not store your card number, expiry date, or card security code. Those details stay with Stripe. Stripe's privacy policy applies to the payment.

Downloads

When you finish paying, Stripe sends your browser to this site with a checkout session id in the page address. We send that id to Stripe to confirm the payment, then give you a short-lived link to the file. We do not write the session id into our own application logs.

To slow abuse, we keep a short-lived counter for the connection. The counter is stored under a hash, not the raw IP address, and it is only used to rate-limit download requests. We do not use it for marketing. A refund stops further downloads of that purchase.

Why we collect it

  • To deliver the download or start the subscription, and to send the receipt
  • To answer support questions and to process refunds and cancellations
  • To keep tax and accounting records of the sale

We do not sell your personal data.

How long we keep it

We keep your name, email, and purchase records for 7 years after the purchase, or for 7 years after a subscription ends. That window covers delivery, the refund period, and tax and accounting duties. After 7 years we delete them, unless the law requires us to keep a record longer. Email you send us is kept for the same period.

relnotes

If you subscribe to relnotes, stored notes generated from repositories you connect are deleted when you cancel, as the relnotes page describes. Your name, email, and the purchase record for that subscription follow the 7-year period above.

Kittie Solitare

The Kittie Solitare app has its own privacy policy. That policy covers the app. This page covers the shop.
