Privacy policy

Last updated: August 15, 2026

Kittie Solitare (“the app”) is a cat-themed Klondike solitaire game for iPhone and iPad.

We do not collect personal information. The app does not require an account, does not include analytics or advertising SDKs, and does not send gameplay data to our servers.

Game progress (current deal, score, daily challenge streak, unlocked themes, and wildcard balance) is stored on your device using Apple’s UserDefaults. Uninstalling the app deletes this data.

Optional in-app purchases are processed by Apple through StoreKit. We do not receive your name, email, or payment card details. Apple’s privacy policy applies to those transactions.

The app does not track you across other companies’ apps or websites. We do not use Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA).

Kittie Solitare is suitable for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. This is not a Kids Category app.

We do not sell, share, or rent user data. The only third party involved in purchases is Apple.

Questions: hello@nixonmade.com or support.
