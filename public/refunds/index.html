This policy is part of the terms of sale. It covers downloadable kits and subscriptions from NixonMade.

Digital kits

You can ask for a full refund of a downloadable kit within 14 days of purchase if you have not substantially used it.

Substantial use means you used the files in real work, or you shared them with other people. Opening the download to see that the files arrived, and opening a file to check that it is not broken, is not substantial use.

Broken files

You can also ask for a refund if a file is broken and we cannot fix it. This applies even after the 14-day window. Email us the file name and what failed. We will try to repair or replace that file. If we cannot, we refund the purchase.

How to ask

Email hello@nixonmade.com from the address you used at checkout. Include the purchase date and, for a broken file, which file failed. We refund through Stripe to the original payment method. We do not store your card number.

Subscriptions

You can cancel relnotes, or any other subscription, at any time. Cancellation stops the next charge. We do not refund a partial month. You keep access through the end of the period you have already paid for. Cancel before the renewal date if you do not want the next charge.

Done-for-you services

The 14-day kit rule covers downloadable files. It does not refund work we have already started. If we have not started a done-for-you service, you can cancel it within 14 days of purchase for a refund. After we have started, the fee covers the work described on the product page. If we do not deliver that work, email us and we will finish it or refund the service fee.
