What it does

  1. Pick which macOS a Mac can run. Stickler shows the versions that Mac supports.
  2. Pick an installer for one of those versions.
  3. Pick the USB drive. Everything on it will be erased.
  4. Write the stick.
  5. Verify it, so you know the stick is good before you need it.

Starting a Mac from the stick

  • Intel Macs: plug in the stick, restart, and hold the Option key until the startup disks appear.
  • Apple silicon Macs: plug in the stick, shut down, then press and hold the power button until startup options appear.
  • Macs with a T2 chip: first allow booting from external media in Startup Security Utility.
