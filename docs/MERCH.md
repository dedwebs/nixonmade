# Nixon Made tees

Print-ready marks already live in `public/brand/`. Do not restyle letterspacing. Upload the outlined SVGs as-is.

No Printful, Printify, Shopify, Gumroad, or Stripe account is wired to this site yet. The shop waitlists via `hello@nixonmade.com` until a partner is connected. Do not add fake buy URLs.

## SKUs

| Slug | Shirt | Mark file | Placement |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `stamp` | Natural / paper cream | `stacked.svg` (square postage stamp, no background) | Center chest, ~7 in wide |
| `press` | Natural / paper cream | `press-stamp.svg` (circular ink-press, ink rings + oxblood type/star, no background) | Center chest, ~8 in, printed ~8° off square |

Do **not** print `*-on-paper.svg` or `*-on-ink.svg` — those include a background rectangle. DTG/screen needs transparent artwork.

Site logo is `press-stamp.svg`. The square postage mark is shirt-only for now.

## Blank

Heavyweight unisex crew, midweight or better (something like a Comfort Colors 1717 / similar). Natural / paper cream. No heather, no neon, no chest pocket.

## When a printer is connected

1. Printful or Printify store + Nixon Made Stripe (or Shopify payments).
2. Create the two products from the table above.
3. Replace each product page’s waitlist mailto with the real product URL.
4. Put that URL in the matching page’s `data-buy-url` attribute (see the tee templates). Leave it empty until then.
